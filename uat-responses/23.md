Hi all – thanks. Summary of the agreed process and where each open question stands:
- ✅ **Who owns invoicing?** Per the agreement (Natalja, 10/21): the **Order Desk** is responsible for accurate order entry, so invoicing runs automatically. Invoices are **not** sent automatically if the sales price is below the purchase price – the Order Desk reviews and sends manually (if OK) or corrects it first. Natalja's work instruction is on SharePoint: *Order Desk – How to monitor the automatic Sales Invoice generation.docx*.

- ✅ The **Customer Invoice Email(s)** must be populated on the customer record or the invoice email won't send.
